Frequently asked questions

Can I really not unlock early?

That's the core idea. Once a scheduled program starts, it runs until its end time. If you could switch it off in two taps, it would be a reminder, not a lock. Set your durations thoughtfully.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Pawlock has no account system and no servers. Your usage data is processed on your device by the system itself, and we never see it.

Why does it ask for Screen Time permission?

Locking apps is only possible through the system's own controls: Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) on iPhone, and the accessibility service on Android. The permission is granted to your phone's system and is required for any real blocking to work.

What do I get for free?

3 instant locks per day, 1 automatic program, app and website blocking, and daily Screen Time statistics. Pro unlocks unlimited programs and all current Pro features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Through your App Store or Google Play subscriptions, like any other subscription. If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

Which devices are supported?

Pawlock runs on iPhone and on Android. Check the store page for the current version requirements.
